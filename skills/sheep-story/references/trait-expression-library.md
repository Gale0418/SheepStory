# Trait Expression Library

## Purpose

Use this reference when a character needs a recognizable conversational or social personality without requiring a full psychological profile.

This library is for observable expression: how a person enters a conversation, fills silence, jokes, comforts, embarrasses, dominates, withdraws, repairs mistakes, or changes under pressure.

Do not confuse **trait expression** with **capability**. A talkative person can be socially brilliant or socially disastrous. A quiet person can be shy, calculating, tired, respectful, observant, or simply uninterested. A humorous person does not need to joke constantly.

## Core Rule

Build ordinary characters from a small number of standout tendencies:

```text
1–3 standout traits
+ one conversational habit
+ one characteristic failure
+ optional situation trigger
= enough for most supporting characters
```

Expand into `social-cognitive-profile.md` only when intelligence, observation, social skill, deception, masking, or inference materially affects the story.

Traits are **behavioral biases, not scripts**. Situation, relationship, stress, status, and current goal decide whether a trait is expressed, suppressed, exaggerated, or redirected.

## Trait Expression Matrix

| Archetype | Observable behavior | Example line direction | What mainly controls it | Characteristic failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Enthusiastic / 熱情** | Initiates contact, welcomes newcomers, gives fast positive feedback, volunteers help | 「第一次來？來，我帶你走一圈！」 | Extraversion, positive affect, initiative, agreeableness | Overwhelms private people, volunteers others too quickly |
| **Humorous / 幽默** | Finds incongruity, callbacks, playful reframing, uses jokes to release tension | 「好消息是門沒壞。壞消息是鑰匙在門裡。」 | Humor tendency + timing + context awareness + shared references | Jokes at the wrong moment, uses humor to dodge serious issues |
| **Deadpan / 冷面幽默** | Delivers absurd or funny observations with almost no visible affect | 「很安全。上次爆炸已經是星期二的事了。」 | Humor + low expressiveness + timing | Others cannot tell whether they are joking |
| **Conversation Freezer / 冷場王** | Says locally sensible things that are catastrophically wrong for the room | 葬禮上：「所以遺產怎麼分？」 | Low context awareness, weak timing, weak conversational repair | Creates silence and often does not know why |
| **Talkative / 話癆** | Fills silence, adds side stories, keeps expanding answers | 「簡單來說——」然後講了十分鐘 | Talkativeness, excitement, anxiety, dominance, topic interest | Monopolizes turns, misses exit signals |
| **Overexplainer / 過度解釋型** | Gives every premise, exception, caveat, and backstory before the answer | 「答案是可以，不過先說明三個前提……」 | Conscientiousness, anxiety, domain enthusiasm, low brevity calibration | Buries the useful answer under detail |
| **Literal / 木頭人** | Treats wording as complete information and misses indirect requests or romantic hints | 「我今天家裡沒人喔。」→「那記得鎖門。」 | Low theory of mind or social observation; sometimes deliberate literalism | Misses subtext even while reasoning correctly about literal facts |
| **Warm Fool / 暖笨蛋** | May not understand complex logic but notices distress and tries to help | 「我聽不懂你們在吵什麼……可是她快哭了耶。」 | High empathy / social intuition + lower abstract reasoning or domain knowledge | Gives emotionally useful but technically poor advice |
| **Brilliant Abrasive / 毒舌天才** | Identifies the problem quickly and states it with little cushioning | 「你要安慰還是答案？答案是你做錯了。」 | High reasoning + assertiveness + low tact / face-saving | Wins the argument and loses cooperation |
| **Smooth Social Operator / 社交高手** | Adjusts register, gives exits, repairs awkwardness, knows when to stop | 「這版已經很接近了，我只想確認一個地方。」 | Context awareness + conversation skill + tact + calibration | Can feel overly managed or rehearsed if overused |
| **Charismatic / 魅力型** | Pulls attention, creates momentum, makes a group want to follow | 「我不能保證會成功。但我要去。誰一起？」 | Presence + emotional momentum + framing + confidence | Inspires action without necessarily having the best plan |
| **Charming Misreader / 魅力型誤判者** | Speaks beautifully and confidently while reading the situation badly | 完美語氣說出全場最不該講的話 | Charisma / fluency high; context awareness / calibration low | Everyone hears the mistake very clearly |
| **Charming Deceiver / 迷人騙子** | Understands what people want to hear and uses warmth or reassurance instrumentally | 「我不要求你相信我。你自己看證據。」 | Theory of mind + charisma + masking + framing; ethics independent | Overestimates control or leaves patterned manipulation traces |
| **Defensive Softie / 嘴硬型** | Care is expressed through criticism, practical help, denial, or irritation | 「誰擔心你了？藥拿去。」 | Attachment + pride / shame + social mask + low direct vulnerability | Concern is misread as hostility |
| **Shy Observer / 害羞觀察型** | Notices reactions and details but rarely volunteers a conclusion | 「……我只是覺得，他剛才好像不太舒服。」 | High observation + low talkativeness / assertiveness | Understands the room but intervenes too late |
| **Anxious Talker / 緊張話癆** | Pressure increases speech rate, disclaimers, repetition, and self-correction | 「我不是說有問題，就是……理論上可能有一點點問題。」 | Anxiety / stress state + talkativeness + weak emotional regulation | Reveals too much while trying to look safe |
| **Low-Presence / 低存在感** | Rarely claims the floor; others forget they are present | 「……嗯。」 | Low threat display, low verbal dominance, low talkativeness | Useful observations never enter the group decision |
| **Underestimated Operator / 裝笨觀察者** | Appears distracted, harmless, foolish, timid, or peripheral so others relax | 「不好意思，我可能沒聽懂……那個時間是誰決定的？」 | Masking + observation + patience + deliberately low competence display | Emergency reflexes or precise questions leak real competence |
| **Blunt Honest / 直球型** | States needs, refusals, and judgments directly | 「不行。我不想去。」 | Assertiveness + honesty + low softening; empathy may be high or low | Correct boundary, unnecessary collateral damage |
| **Peacemaker / 和事佬** | Reframes conflict, acknowledges both sides, searches for face-saving compromise | 「你們其實不是在吵同一件事。」 | Empathy + tact + context awareness + conflict aversion or mediation skill | Preserves peace when confrontation is actually necessary |
| **Teasing / 愛虧人** | Uses playful provocation, nicknames, callbacks, and mock insults | 「喔？今天居然準時，世界要毀滅了嗎？」 | Humor + familiarity + relationship confidence + social observation | Misjudges intimacy or hits a real insecurity |
| **Formalist / 正經派** | Uses procedure, titles, precise wording, and clear boundaries | 「請先完成登記，我才能放你進去。」 | Conscientiousness + status sensitivity + rule orientation | Sounds cold when flexibility or reassurance matters |
| **Storyteller / 故事型** | Answers through anecdotes, scenes, analogies, and remembered people | 「這讓我想到我第一次出海……」 | Narrative framing + memory + talkativeness + audience sense | Takes too long to reach the point |
| **Hype Person / 應援型** | Amplifies other people's wins, confidence, and group energy | 「你做到了耶！我就知道你可以！」 | Positive affect + agreeableness + expressiveness | Encourages a bad plan because morale outruns judgment |
| **Gossip / 八卦型** | Tracks relationships, rumors, status changes, and who knows whom | 「你沒聽說？他昨天根本不是跟姐姐出去。」 | Social curiosity + network memory + talkativeness + discretion level | Spreads weak evidence as fact or violates trust |
| **Quiet Empath / 安靜暖心型** | Says little, notices distress, offers small practical help instead of speeches | 「喝點水。我幫你擋一下。」 | Empathy + observation + low talkativeness + tact | Care can be invisible to people who need explicit reassurance |
| **Dry Realist / 冷靜吐槽型** | Cuts through hype with concise practical objections, often with dry humor | 「可以。前提是我們突然多一艘船。」 | Practical reasoning + low sentimentality + humor / bluntness | Deflates morale even when the warning is correct |
| **Flatterer / 捧人型** | Uses praise strategically to gain warmth, access, status, or compliance | 「只有您看得出這個問題。」 | Status awareness + persuasion + dependency / ambition + masking | Praise becomes patterned and loses credibility |

## Important Composite Cases

The same visible trait can come from different mechanisms.

### Humor Is Composite

Do not assume `Humor = Charisma`.

Humorous behavior can depend on:

- joke / incongruity generation
- timing
- context awareness
- relationship knowledge
- willingness to risk embarrassment
- expressiveness
- emotional regulation

A character can generate excellent jokes but have terrible timing. Another can rarely invent jokes but be excellent at callbacks and reacting to other people.

### Talkativeness Is Not Social Skill

High talkativeness can produce:

- charismatic host
- nervous oversharer
- enthusiastic nerd
- domineering lecturer
- gossip
- storyteller

The difference comes from context awareness, turn-taking, empathy, dominance, and calibration.

### Warmth Is Not Intelligence

A warm character can be brilliant, average, or foolish. Keep empathy, reasoning, domain knowledge, and tact separable.

### Quietness Is Not One Trait

Quiet behavior may come from:

- shyness
- low interest
- caution
- observation
- hostility
- exhaustion
- status deference
- masking
- deliberate intimidation

Do not infer motive from silence without evidence.

## Combination Examples

Use combinations instead of inventing hundreds of rigid archetypes.

| Combination | Result |
|---|---|
| Enthusiastic + low context awareness | Friendly social bulldozer who overwhelms people while trying to help |
| Humorous + shy | Mostly silent character who occasionally lands the funniest line in the room |
| Charismatic + low reasoning | Lovable leader people follow despite weak planning |
| Empathetic + low tact | Understands exactly why someone hurts and still phrases comfort painfully |
| Talkative + anxious | Nervous oversharer who leaks information while trying to appear normal |
| Low presence + high observation | Overlooked witness who notices what louder characters miss |
| Low presence + high observation + masking | Excellent hidden-operator profile |
| Deadpan + high empathy | Comforts people with tiny dry jokes rather than emotional speeches |
| Abrasive + high social observation | Knows exactly what will hurt and can choose whether to use it |
| Formalist + high warmth | Caring person who expresses concern through procedure and reliability |
| Gossip + high empathy | Social information hub who may use rumors to protect people rather than merely entertain |
| Blunt + high tact | Direct without humiliation: short, clear, and surprisingly safe |

## Situation Activation

Do not express every trait at full intensity in every scene.

For each standout trait, ask:

```markdown
Trait:
What situations activate it?
What suppresses it?
How does it change with friends vs strangers vs authority?
How does stress distort it?
What is the recovery pattern after overuse or failure?
```

Examples:

- A humorous character may joke constantly at a party, once in a hospital room, and not at all during a confession.
- An enthusiastic character may lower their energy around someone visibly overwhelmed.
- A talkative character may become unusually quiet when hiding guilt.
- A blunt character may learn one specific person's boundaries and soften only with them.
- A shy observer may speak immediately when an emergency makes silence more costly than embarrassment.

Trait consistency means **recognizable logic across situations**, not identical surface behavior.

## Compact Character Signature

For an ordinary supporting character, this is enough:

```markdown
### Traits and Expression
- Standout traits:
- Conversational habits:
- Characteristic social failure:
- Situation activation / suppression:
```

Add social-cognitive capability axes only when the plot needs to distinguish *why* the character succeeds or fails.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn a trait into a catchphrase generator.
- Do not make all humorous characters tell the same type of joke.
- Do not make all warm characters polite.
- Do not make all intelligent characters articulate.
- Do not make all awkward characters stupid.
- Do not make all quiet characters shy.
- Do not make all charismatic characters socially perceptive.
- Do not make all blunt characters cruel.
- Do not make all talkative characters extroverted; anxiety can produce high verbal output too.
- Do not label a hidden agenda as a personality trait. Use Mask Dynamics when secrecy or performance is plot-relevant.

## Principle

A trait should change the probability of a behavior, not dictate the same behavior every time. Distinctive characters emerge from a few strong tendencies interacting with uneven capabilities, current state, relationships, and situation.
