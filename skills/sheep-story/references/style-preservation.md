# Style Preservation

## Purpose

Style preservation prevents revision from destroying the author's voice.

Polishing should improve clarity, pressure, rhythm, and specificity without flattening the prose into generic assistant output.

## Protect These

Default to preserving:

- distinctive sentence rhythm
- deliberate repetition
- character-specific word choice
- awkwardness that reveals character
- silence and omission
- ambiguity
- narrator bias
- sensory habits
- recurring motifs
- rough emotional texture
- unusual but intentional phrasing

## Safe to Change

Usually safe:

- typos
- broken punctuation
- accidental repetition
- paragraph breaks that obscure meaning
- unclear pronoun reference
- inconsistent tense when not intentional
- mechanical transition words
- generic summary that can become visible action

## Dangerous to Change

Be careful with:

- dialogue rhythm
- inner monologue
- flirtation
- hostility
- evasive speech
- unreliable narration
- scene-level ambiguity
- intentionally ugly or blunt sentences
- repeated images that may be motif rather than mistake

## Specialist Escalation

Use the snapshot as a routing tool, not a demand to normalize the prose.

- If the problem is **who owns perception, head-hopping, impossible access, filter mediation, free-indirect diction, or an unmotivated change in closeness**, read `narrative-distance-and-pov.md`.
- If the problem is **sentence or paragraph cadence, clause shape, landing emphasis, repetition, breath, punctuation tempo, or a mechanical paragraph pulse**, read `prose-rhythm.md`.

Do not load either specialist just because the snapshot contains `POV distance` and `Sentence rhythm`. Escalate only when the passage's effect materially depends on that dial.

A specialist diagnosis does not authorize universal deep POV or universal rhythm variation. Preserve intentional distance, pullbacks, monotone, repetition, fragments, silence, and useful roughness when they serve the passage.

These specialists operate at the expression layer. Do not convert a sentence-level POV-distance or cadence choice into durable canon unless the underlying fact itself changes.

## Revision Method

1. Identify what the passage is trying to do.
2. Identify which texture carries voice.
3. Fix only what interferes with the scene's effect.
4. Preserve character-bearing imperfections.
5. Run anti-AI-flavour without over-polishing.
6. Escalate to the POV-distance or prose-rhythm specialist only when the local problem survives the basic pass.

## Anti-Sterilization Check

After revision, ask:

- Does this still sound like the same narrator?
- Did the characters become too polite?
- Did ambiguity disappear unnecessarily?
- Did the prose become too evenly paced?
- Did POV distance become uniformly close even where the original deliberately pulled back?
- Did deliberate monotone, refrain, or repetition become artificial sentence variety?
- Did rough emotion turn into elegant explanation?
- Could any assistant have written this version?

If yes, restore texture.

Do not turn a living draft into a clean corpse.
