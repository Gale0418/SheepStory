# Pacing, Reveal, and Extensions

## Advisory Pacing and Reveal

Pacing and reveal analysis is advisory. Inspect scene function, pressure variation, information timing, reader orientation, emotional consequence, recovery, and deliberate stillness. Explain the evidence and competing readings. Do not force a beat, climax, reveal, dialogue ratio, promise payoff rate, or word-count target at a fixed interval; there are no fixed KPIs.

Reveal advice must distinguish what the reader knows, what a character knows, what is inferred, and what remains unknown. A recommendation may suggest earlier setup, a delayed answer, or a clearer transition, but it cannot declare a reveal canon or erase a productive ambiguity.

## Export Boundary

An export carries a bounded source snapshot, selected context, advisory findings, unresolved risks, and requested destination. It must not silently include quarantined material, private notes, or unapproved alternates. Export is a prompt or document handoff, not a canon write.

## Branching Boundary

Branches are labeled candidates with a parent snapshot, invariant facts, changed choices, continuity effects, and merge decision. A branch remains isolated until the owner explicitly selects and promotes it through the normal approval and memory process. Branch analysis must preserve multiple plausible readings instead of treating one branch as hidden truth.

## Autonomous Extension Boundary

An autonomous extension may draft only inside an explicit contract: scope, source snapshot, capability ceiling, style constraints, allowed new facts, stop condition, and review destination. It must stop before canon mutation, new exceptional capability, unapproved relationship or timeline change, or unresolved contradiction. “Continue” is not blanket authorization to invent project truth.

## Context Budget and Epistemic Limits

Record a context budget and the sources actually loaded. If relevant state did not fit, mark the result partial and request a smaller scope or more context; never fill the gap with confident invention. Structural parsing can identify headings, links, metadata, and repeated labels, but parser output is not evidence of authorial intent, reader consensus, canon, or truth.

## Review

Reviewers may accept, revise, defer, or reject an advisory. The decision records evidence, dissent, affected promises and events, and any authorized canonical destination. Keep the advisory and branch trace for audit without duplicating canonical state.
