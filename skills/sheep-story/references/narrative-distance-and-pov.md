# Narrative Distance and POV

## Purpose

Use this reference when a passage's effect depends on **who owns perception, how close the narration sits to a viewpoint character, what information the prose may legitimately reveal, or how the narrative lens moves**.

Narrative distance is a craft dial, not a quality score. Closer is not automatically better. A remote, cool, documentary, ironic, ceremonial, or panoramic passage can be exactly right. The requirement is control.

Keep these separable:

```text
grammatical person
!= POV ownership
!= narrative distance
!= narrator knowledge
!= character knowledge
!= reader knowledge
```

First person can be emotionally remote. Third person can be deeply interior. Omniscient narration can still choose restraint.

## Intent First

Before diagnosing distance, ask what the moment is trying to do.

Possible intents include:

- establish place or scale
- withhold interiority
- create intimacy
- make perception feel immediate
- preserve uncertainty
- expose bias or self-deception
- create ironic distance
- enter an altered state
- let a revelation land internally
- pull back after shock
- hand the scene to another viewpoint
- deliberately break distance for a formal effect

Do not force a passage toward maximum intimacy because "deep POV" sounds advanced. Judge whether the chosen distance serves the moment and whether changes are deliberate.

## POV Ownership

POV ownership answers:

> Whose perceptual and interpretive access governs this passage right now?

For a limited viewpoint, separate:

```text
what the viewpoint character can directly sense
what they remember
what they know
what they infer
what they suspect
what they misread
what the reader knows from elsewhere
```

Do not convert reader knowledge or another character's private thought into the viewpoint character's direct access.

### Observable Is Not Omniscient

A limited viewpoint may describe another character's:

- words
- visible movement
- expression
- posture
- breath
- hesitation
- touch
- object handling
- publicly known facts
- environmental traces

It may infer motive or emotion when the inference is framed as the viewpoint character's inference.

It may not silently assert another person's unspoken private thought, hidden memory, unseen action, or secret knowledge as direct fact unless the narrative mode explicitly grants that access.

Weak limited-POV move:

```text
Mina smiled. She secretly hoped he would leave before he noticed the letter.
```

If the current viewpoint belongs to someone who cannot know Mina's private hope, this is a head-hop or omniscient assertion.

Limited alternative direction:

```text
Mina smiled too quickly and slid the letter under her wrist.
```

The reader may infer. The viewpoint character may suspect. Neither automatically knows.

## Narrative Distance as a Dial

Do not treat distance as a rigid universal ladder. Use these broad diagnostic bands only when they help locate a problem.

### 1. Panoramic

The narration stands far enough back to emphasize:

- era
- place
- group
- historical scale
- pattern
- public consequence
- external action over private thought

Interiority is sparse or absent.

### 2. Observational

The narration follows a specific character but remains substantially outside.

Common markers:

- visible action
- summarized disposition
- mediated perception
- restrained interiority
- narrator diction remains distinct

### 3. Close

The narration shares the viewpoint character's evaluations and immediate mental framing.

Common markers:

- direct perception with less mediation
- judgments colored by the character
- thoughts integrated into narration
- selective sensory focus
- diction beginning to belong to the viewpoint character

### 4. Deep Interior

The narration is strongly shaped by the character's immediate consciousness.

Common markers:

- raw sensation
- associative thought
- fragments or compressed syntax when character-appropriate
- free-indirect or near-unmediated diction
- little need to explain that the character is perceiving or thinking

Deep interiority is strong medicine, not the default destination of every paragraph.

## Distance Markers

Use multiple markers together. Never classify distance from one word.

### Perception Mediation

Compare:

```text
She saw the red warning light above the hatch.
```

with:

```text
Red. Above the hatch.
```

The second can feel closer because perception lands directly, but it is not automatically better.

A perceiving verb may be useful when the act of noticing itself matters:

```text
Only then did she notice the second light.
```

Now the delay in attention is story information.

### Interiority

Distance can move through:

```text
external action
→ named feeling or thought
→ character-colored interpretation
→ thought/sensation as it forms
```

Do not replace every emotional statement with body language. Sometimes the direct naming of a thought or feeling is exactly the intended distance.

### Diction Source

At close distance, ask:

> Whose words are these?

If a teenage mechanic's close interior narration suddenly uses detached institutional vocabulary they would never think in, the prose may be physically close but linguistically distant.

Free-indirect narration works when narrator and character language intentionally converge without becoming impossible knowledge.

### Syntax and Compression

Syntax can contribute to distance:

- complete, explanatory sentences may hold some remove
- fragments may create immediacy
- associative jumps may deepen interiority
- summary can pull outward
- long reflective syntax can still be close when it belongs to the character

Do not use fragments as a universal "deep POV" costume.

## Filter Verbs Are Not Banned

Potential filter verbs include:

```text
saw
heard
felt
noticed
watched
realized
wondered
knew
seemed

看見
聽見
感到
注意到
發現
意識到
想著
覺得
知道
```

They can add distance by explicitly naming the act of perception.

Do **not** delete them mechanically.

Keep them when they carry:

- attention shift
- uncertainty
- delayed noticing
- contrast between perception and reality
- self-conscious thought
- deliberate observational distance
- timing
- unreliable perception

Reduce them when:

- the scene intends close immediacy
- the perceptual act adds no information
- repeated mediation creates a fog between reader and experience

A filter verb is evidence, not a conviction.

## Distance Movement

A scene may intentionally move:

```text
wide → close
close → deep
deep → close
close → wide
```

Useful reasons include:

- establishing geography before emotion
- moving inward as danger becomes personal
- entering memory or panic
- pulling outward after shock
- broadening from private consequence to public consequence
- ending on an image after intense interiority

A distance change should usually have a perceptible dramatic reason.

### Distance Drift

Drift is an unmotivated change in closeness.

Example pattern:

```text
close character-colored perception
→ generic narrator explanation
→ deep fragment
→ external summary
```

when nothing in the scene motivates the movement.

The problem is not that multiple distances appear. The problem is loss of control.

### Head-Hop vs Intentional Handoff

Head-hopping is not synonymous with "more than one POV exists."

A problematic head-hop usually:

- enters another character's private interiority without transition
- changes information authority mid-beat
- makes it unclear who owns perception
- grants access the established viewpoint mode did not allow

Intentional handoff may be valid at:

- chapter boundary
- section break
- clearly staged scene transition
- an established omniscient movement
- a deliberate formal cut

A neutral or more observational bridge can make a handoff easier to read, but it is not a magical requirement. Clarity and established narrative rules matter more than ritual procedure.

## Free-Indirect and Character-Colored Narration

Free-indirect narration lets character language color third-person narration without constant thought tags.

Check:

- vocabulary
- metaphors
- judgments
- attention
- omissions
- exaggerations
- rhythm

The prose should reveal the character's mental texture without falsely granting them knowledge they do not possess.

Character-colored narration is not permission to stereotype a voice. Use established language and perception habits rather than generic slang or forced quirks.

## Narrative Distance and AI Flavour

AI-flavoured prose often fails at distance even when every sentence is grammatical.

### Generic Hovering Narrator

The narration floats at the same medium distance regardless of danger, intimacy, discovery, or shock.

Symptoms:

- every perception is explained
- every emotion is summarized
- narrator diction remains generic
- no beat changes the lens

### Emotion Label Conveyor

Pattern:

```text
event
→ "she felt..."
→ explanation of why
→ action
```

repeated across the passage.

The issue is not that emotion labels are forbidden. It is the mechanical recurrence.

### Fake Deep POV

Symptoms:

- lots of fragments
- lots of raw sensation
- no character-specific diction
- impossible knowledge remains
- narrator explanations still appear between "deep" lines

Deep POV is not punctuation cosplay.

### Camera Teleport

The prose jumps:

- inside one character
- into another character's private thought
- to a detached global fact
- back into the first character

without an established omniscient mode or transition.

### Explanatory Pull-Out

An emotionally close beat lands, then the narration immediately explains what it means.

This often drains the beat:

```text
close reaction
→ explanatory moral / motive summary
```

Sometimes reflection is needed. Ask whether the explanation belongs to the viewpoint voice and whether the distance shift serves the scene.

## Revision Protocol

When revising POV or distance:

1. **Name the current owner.**
   - Who is allowed perceptual or interior access?

2. **Name the intended distance or movement.**
   - Hold, glide in, glide out, hard cut, or handoff?

3. **Mark unauthorized knowledge.**
   - Another person's thought? Reader-only fact? Offstage information?

4. **Mark distance evidence.**
   - filter mediation
   - narrator diction
   - character diction
   - interiority
   - syntax
   - summary
   - sensory immediacy

5. **Fix the smallest causal problem.**
   - reframe private fact as observation/inference
   - remove unnecessary mediation
   - restore character diction
   - add a transition
   - remove an explanatory pull-out

6. **Preserve deliberate distance.**
   - do not make every sentence closer
   - do not erase a purposeful pullback
   - do not convert limited POV into omniscience to make revision easier

7. **Recheck meaning and continuity.**
   - craft revision must not invent canon or change what characters truly know.

## Review Failure Modes

### Head-Hop

Private access changes owners without an established transition.

### Impossible Perception

The viewpoint character senses, sees, hears, remembers, or knows something unavailable from their position or epistemic state.

### Distance Drift

Closeness changes without dramatic purpose.

### Filter Fog

Repeated mediation keeps the reader farther away than the intended moment requires.

### Fake Closeness

Fragments, italics, or sensory words simulate depth while diction and explanation remain generic.

### Constant Maximum Intimacy

Every beat is forced into deep interiority, flattening contrast and exhausting the reader.

### Mechanical Pullback

Every emotional beat is followed by explanation or summary.

### Person-Distance Collapse

First person is assumed to be deep, or third person is assumed to be distant.

### Omniscience-by-Repair

A limited-POV problem is "fixed" by casually granting the narrator unlimited access, even though the project's narrative mode did not change.

## Quick Audit

Ask:

```markdown
Whose perception owns this passage?
What can that viewpoint legitimately know here?
What distance does the moment want?
What words make it feel that distance?
Does the diction belong to the viewpoint character or a generic narrator?
Are filter verbs doing narrative work or merely mediating?
Does any distance shift have a dramatic reason?
Did I accidentally enter another person's private interiority?
Did I preserve deliberate pullbacks, summary, or distance?
Did the revision change story facts or epistemic state?
```

## Principle

Control the lens.

Do not confuse access with omniscience, closeness with quality, or grammatical person with distance.
