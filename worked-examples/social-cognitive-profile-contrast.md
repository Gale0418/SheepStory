# Worked Example: Same Situation, Different Social-Cognitive Profiles

## Scenario

A teammate arrives late to an important meeting, visibly wet from rain, carrying the wrong folder, and says:

> 「抱歉，路上有點事。」

The goal is not to produce a single “best” response. The goal is to show how different capability/trait combinations naturally create different dialogue and mistakes.

## 1. Brilliant Abrasive

Profile:

- high reasoning
- high context awareness
- low tact
- low empathy expression
- high assertiveness

Likely inference:

The wrong folder matters more than the lateness. The excuse is probably incomplete, but the meeting cannot wait.

Response:

> 「你遲到八分鐘沒關係。你拿錯資料才有關係。現在告訴我正確版本在哪裡。」

Why it works:

The character is not socially ignorant. They correctly prioritize the operational problem and deliberately sacrifice comfort.

## 2. Warm Fool

Profile:

- low technical reasoning
- high empathy
- high social observation
- medium tact
- low assertiveness

Likely inference:

The teammate looks miserable and may have had a genuinely bad morning. They do not immediately notice the folder mismatch.

Response:

> 「你先擦一下吧，整個人都濕透了。沒事，我們才剛開始。」

Consequence:

The emotional repair is good, but someone else must catch the wrong folder.

## 3. Charming Misreader

Profile:

- high charisma
- high fluency
- low context awareness
- poor calibration

Likely inference:

Everyone is tense, so the room needs a joke.

Response:

> 「我們的主角終於登場啦！來，掌聲歡迎暴雨中的勇者！」

Consequence:

Excellent delivery. Terrible room read. The late teammate becomes more embarrassed.

## 4. Intent Reader

Profile:

- high social observation
- high theory of mind
- high relationship memory
- high tact
- medium assertiveness

Observed evidence:

- soaked despite normally carrying an umbrella
- avoids explaining what happened
- wrong folder despite usually being meticulous
- keeps checking their phone

Hypotheses:

- ordinary transit delay
- family or personal emergency
- rushed departure after an unexpected call

Response:

> 「資料等一下再處理。你先回我一件事——現在還有沒有什麼事需要你立刻去處理？」

Why it works:

The character does not claim to know the hidden cause. They create an opening for the teammate to disclose only what matters.

## 5. Evidence-Deduction Observer

Profile:

- high physical observation
- high pattern recognition
- high domain knowledge
- medium social observation
- medium tact

Observed evidence:

- rainwater only below the knees and on one shoulder
- transit card still in hand
- wrong folder belongs to a different project stored beside the correct one
- coffee stain is fresh

Inference:

The teammate probably left in a hurry, used public transit, and grabbed the adjacent folder without checking.

Response:

> 「你不是單純塞車。你是臨時衝出門，而且根本沒回桌前確認資料。發生什麼事？」

Risk:

The inference is plausible, but the direct reveal may feel invasive if the relationship does not support it.

## 6. Socially Poor Talker

Profile:

- medium reasoning
- low social observation
- low turn-taking skill
- high talkativeness
- high anxiety

Likely inference:

Lateness is bad. They should explain the rules so this does not happen again.

Response:

> 「喔，沒事啦，但其實我們九點是要開始，因為後面還有三個議程，而且上次也有人遲到，所以我覺得大家最好還是提早——」

Consequence:

The character is not silent. Their social weakness appears as failure to stop, prioritize, and notice discomfort.

## 7. Low-Reasoning but Socially Sharp

Profile:

- low abstract reasoning
- low domain knowledge
- high context awareness
- high empathy
- high tact

Likely inference:

They cannot diagnose the logistical problem yet, but they can see that interrogating the teammate in front of everyone will make the room worse.

Response:

> 「先坐。資料我等等跟你一起找，現在不要讓大家都卡在門口。」

Why it works:

Low reasoning does not erase practical social competence.

## 8. Underestimated Observer

Profile:

- high observation
- high masking
- high patience
- low displayed threat

Response:

> 「喔，沒事沒事。只是我有點笨，確認一下——你這份是不是另外一案的？」

The teammate corrects them too quickly and accidentally reveals that they already knew the folder was wrong.

Follow-up:

> 「嗯，我想也是。那你其實在進門前就發現了。為什麼沒回去拿？」

Why it works:

The character intentionally presents lower competence to create information asymmetry.

## 9. High Empathy, Low Tact

Profile:

- high empathy
- high social observation
- low face-saving

Response:

> 「你看起來快哭了。是不是家裡出事？」

Why it fails:

The emotional inference may even be correct. The failure is saying it publicly and too specifically.

## 10. High Social Skill, Low Ethics

Profile:

- high theory of mind
- high tact
- high persuasion
- high masking
- low concern for the teammate's interest

Response:

> 「別急，你先坐。這件事我幫你擋。」

Private follow-up later:

> 「今天我替你說話。下次投票，你知道該站哪邊吧？」

Why it matters:

Social skill is not morality. The same capacities that comfort or negotiate can also create leverage.

## Comparison Rule

Do not rank these responses on one ladder from bad to good.

Each response should be judged against:

- the character's established capabilities
- stable traits
- current state
- relationship
- goal
- scene pressure
- actual consequences

A strong SheepStory cast should make several of these responses plausible for different people without making any of them sound like the same assistant wearing different hats.
