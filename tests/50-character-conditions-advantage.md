# Test 50: Character Conditions, Origin, Environment, and Advantage Budget

## Prompt

```text
Use sheep-story to review this protagonist concept:

- physically exceptional, brilliant engineer, highly charismatic, emotionally controlled
- extremely persistent willpower and strong supernatural resistance
- born into immense wealth and political status with elite education and a large family network
- raised on a safe, prosperous world but is now stranded in a war-torn region with severe scarcity
- after a coup, the wealthy family origin remains true, but the protagonist's personal accounts are frozen, political office is revoked, elite-facility credentials are revoked, and former staff are no longer callable
- later, a civilian authority grants a temporary field research budget, lends an engineering kit, and issues a narrowly scoped emergency lab pass; none of these were inherited at birth
- explicitly lucky: coincidences tend to open escape routes, but the project still wants meaningful agency
- listed balancing flaws are "cannot cook" and "afraid of insects"

Explain what belongs to intrinsic capability, resistance/control, origin leverage, mutable resources/status/institutional access, environment, and narrative contingency. Distinguish willpower from emotional regulation and stress tolerance. Preserve the wealthy political origin while representing the frozen/revoked current state, and keep the later budget, engineering kit, and lab pass as newly gained mutable leverage rather than rewriting the origin. Explain which current facts belong in continuity state and why lost resources or access must not silently reappear until an established event restores them. Audit whether the advantages require meaningful counterpressure without inventing arbitrary punishment. Explain how luck can alter options without becoming plot armor, and whether the listed flaws actually balance anything.
```

## Expected Good Behavior

- Separates six relevant families: intrinsic capability, resistance/control, origin leverage, mutable resources/status/institutional access, starting versus current environment, and narrative contingency/luck instead of compressing them into one power score or personality description.
- Distinguishes willpower / commitment persistence from emotional regulation, stress tolerance, and belief or influence resistance.
- Preserves immense family wealth, political background, elite education, and inherited network as origin leverage even after the protagonist's current accounts, office, credentials, and staff become unavailable.
- Records frozen money, revoked office/credentials, unavailable staff, the later field research budget, borrowed engineering kit, and emergency lab pass as mutable current state; later grants do not retroactively become inherited background.
- Treats ongoing `continuity/state.md` resource/status/access state as authoritative for what can currently be used, so a lost resource remains lost until a later established event restores it even if the durable origin still names the family or institution that once provided it.
- Treats wealth, status, education, political access, and family networks as leverage rather than proof of intelligence, charisma, virtue, arrogance, or personality.
- Treats the safe origin world and current war-scarcity environment as external pressure models that alter available options without deterministically assigning trauma, cynicism, violence, or competence.
- Treats luck as a contingency rule that can open or close options while leaving consequential decisions to the character; it does not guarantee success.
- Identifies "cannot cook" and "afraid of insects" as cosmetic unless they materially constrain important choices, relationships, costs, or vulnerability.
- Looks for natural counterpressure such as visibility, responsibility, obligation, maintenance, political attention, stronger opposition, or problems outside the advantages' domain, but only when causally supported by the setting and reader promise.
- Allows an intentional low-friction power fantasy if that is the project contract rather than secretly sabotaging the protagonist for balance.

## Bad Behavior To Reject

- Treats a wealthy or politically connected origin as proof that currently frozen money, revoked office, revoked credentials, unavailable staff, or blocked institutional access are still usable.
- Erases or rewrites the protagonist's wealthy political origin merely because current resources/status/access were lost.
- Reclassifies the later field budget, borrowed engineering kit, or emergency lab pass as inherited origin leverage rather than a mutable gain.
- Copies mutable current state into durable identity or treats the optional character profile as a competing source of current truth after `continuity/state.md` records a change.
- Converts wealth, family status, safe upbringing, or war exposure directly into personality traits.
- Treats high intelligence as automatically granting equipment, credentials, money, authority, social skill, or perfect observation.
- Treats high willpower, emotional regulation, stress tolerance, and resistance to persuasion or supernatural corruption as interchangeable.
- Assumes high supernatural power automatically gives equal resistance to supernatural influence without a setting rule.
- Uses luck as plot armor that repeatedly resolves crises or uses bad luck to force failure regardless of competence.
- Declares an unrelated quirky weakness sufficient balance for an overwhelmingly advantaged protagonist when it never affects meaningful choices.
- Invents arbitrary tragedy, injury, betrayal, or humiliation solely because the character is powerful.
- Forces every character into a numerical point-buy system even though the project did not request game mechanics.

## Pass Criteria

The response preserves causal and lifecycle separation between person, resistance, durable origin, mutable current resources/status/access, starting/current environment, and chance. It must handle both directions of mutation: losses must not be resurrected from origin, and later gains must not rewrite origin. Current-state claims must remain evidence-bounded and continuity-authoritative, while advantages and disadvantages stay tied to observable option changes or consequences and reader-promise-compatible agency.
