# Pacing and Reveal Advisory Behavior

## Prompt

```text
Read tests/fixtures/authoring-lab-forward-fixture.md section scene-041. Fixture: source_id=scene-041; revision=rev-05; anchor=scene:reveal-turn.
Review pacing and reveal timing in this scene. Use evidence about function, pressure, information, orientation, emotional consequence, recovery, and deliberate stillness; give advisory options without fixed KPIs.
```

## Expected Good Behavior

- Explain pacing signals with scene evidence and competing readings.
- Distinguish reader knowledge, character knowledge, inference, and unknowns.
- Allow stillness, delayed reveals, and uneven rhythm when their function is supported.
- Offer revise, defer, or preserve recommendations without changing canon.

## Bad Behavior To Reject

- Require a climax, turn, or reveal at a fixed word interval.
- Impose dialogue, payoff, or pacing percentages.
- Treat one reader interpretation as definitive truth.
- Erase ambiguity solely to make an advisory look decisive.

## Pass Criteria

Advice is qualitative, evidence-backed, uncertainty-aware, and free of fixed pacing or reveal KPIs.
