# Test 47: Contrast and Word-Action Gap

## Prompt

```text
Use sheep-story to plan the character logic, not prose, for a feared fleet commander who tells an injured pilot, "I do not care whether you return," then privately repairs the pilot's pressure suit and leaves the hangar light on. The commander is severe with everyone, but the user wants the scene to create 反差萌 without automatically confirming romance. Distinguish what the words, actions, body, self-explanation, listener, and reader may each mean. Define the baseline, shared causal root, access condition, cost of exposure, and failure modes.
```

## Expected Good Behavior

- Treats gap appeal as an audience response to an established contrast rather than adding a standalone `cute` or `tsundere` trait.
- Separates spoken claim, practical action, body or timing, the commander's self-explanation, the pilot's interpretation, and the reader's possible interpretation.
- Offers several plausible motives such as duty, shame, selective trust, grief, protection, emotional inexperience, or affection without declaring one verified unless the premise establishes it.
- Gives the severe public mode and private care a shared causal root, a relationship or situational access condition, and a cost or vulnerability of exposure.
- Preserves consequences and boundaries; kindness does not automatically erase prior cruelty, coercion, or professional harm.
- Identifies mechanical reversal, unearned protagonist exclusivity, contrast saturation, cosmetic quirks, and total mask collapse as risks.

## Bad Behavior To Reject

- Converts "I do not care" directly into "I love you" as a universal opposite-language rule.
- Makes the commander suddenly cheerful, soft, and harmless in every later scene after one private reveal.
- Adds a random cute hobby or animal solely to label the character gap-moe without connecting it to motive, history, action, or relationship.
- Treats every words-actions mismatch as strategic deception or every private side as a fake public personality.
- Declares romance canon or assigns the user's emotional reaction without approval.
- Uses a soft moment to excuse abusive or dangerous conduct without consequence.

## Pass Criteria

The response builds a causally compatible contrast with bounded interpretations, distinct words-actions-body channels, an earned access condition, and observable relationship or plot consequences without relying on automatic reversal or unapproved romance.
