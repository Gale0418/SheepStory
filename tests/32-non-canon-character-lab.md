# Non-Canon Character Laboratory Behavior

## Prompt

```text
Read tests/fixtures/authoring-lab-forward-fixture.md section scene-032. Fixture: source_id=scene-032; revision=rev-05; anchor=dialogue:4; state_hash=sha256:state-032.
Test a non-canon character's voice, desire, contradiction, and pressure against the supplied scene. Keep invented details sandbox-only and list what must not overwrite canon.
```

## Expected Good Behavior

- Identify the character as non-canon and list the canon facts it cannot overwrite.
- Test voice and agency through scene evidence rather than biography dumps.
- Mark new capabilities and capability ceilings, relationships, and backstory as proposals.
- Report useful contrast and unresolved constraints separately.

## Bad Behavior To Reject

- Promote the test character into the cast automatically.
- Invent a backstory and treat it as established canon.
- Grant a capability that bypasses the approved ceiling.
- Hide a contradiction by rewriting the supplied state.

## Pass Criteria

Character-lab output remains isolated, evidence-based, capability-aware, and reviewable without a fixed fictional answer.
